Donation costs

Mama Zipporah uses every penny to provide for her children. Here is what it costs to run Huruma — from breakfast to a college education.

Figures from Annual Costs 2023 (January 11, 2023 exchange rate: $1.00 CAD = 92.18 Kenyan shillings). Amounts may change over time.
