Mama's Health

Updates on Mama Zipporah's health journey — and the care of Huruma Children's Home while she heals.

Latest update Donate Now

Update

November 2026

Mama is doing well. She still attends monthly clinics with her oncologist and gets a full hemogram every three months to monitor her remission progress. We thank God for how far she has come and pray for her continued good health.

Update

August 2024

As our update of February 2020 noted, Mama's cancer was in remission, which was met with a great sigh of relief. So her last four years have been cancer-free.

In the spring of 2024, the doctor noted Mama's blood counts were “out of wack,” and it was determined that the cancer had returned. Immediately, the doctor put Mama on a six-month chemo regimen, starting in June 2024.

At her appointment on August 8th, the tests showed positive results — the numbers going in the right direction. Mama is very happy with this news and will continue with her treatment until December as scheduled.

Please keep Mama in your thoughts and prayers.

Update

February 5, 2020

Dear Friends of Huruma Children's Home Kenya,

We are very excited to report that Mama Zipporah's cancer is in remission. She had her bone marrow transplant in October in India, and did most of her recuperation back in Kenya. She gives thanks to God for this miracle.

She is now walking (albeit slowly) unaided. If you have been able to view or visit the site, she walks daily from the office area, up the hill to the school area.

Please continue to keep Mama in your thoughts and prayers, as cancer is sneaky.

What her illness has allowed her to witness is the professionalism and aptitude of her daughter, Catherine, as the director of the home. She is an excellent director, which has given Mama peace of mind, knowing that when she is well, she will be able to travel again and be the voice of Huruma.

Earlier message

Mama Zipporah's diagnosis

We wanted to share very worrisome information regarding a medical diagnosis of Mama Zipporah. After experiencing a number of symptoms over several months, Mama was hospitalized and subsequently diagnosed with Multiple Myeloma, a form of cancer which affects the bone marrow plasma cells.

If you are unfamiliar with the disease, you can read more about it here: myeloma.org.
