A registered Canadian charity

In April 2014, the Canada Revenue Agency granted Canadian charitable status to Huruma Children's Trust (Canada). This allows all donations to receive a taxable receipt for Canadian income tax purposes (830771770RR0001).

We have a formal board of directors. All members of the board have travelled to Huruma Children's Home in the past. Thank you for truly making a difference.
